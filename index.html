Research interests

Looking closer at
the nuclear pore

I’m interested in FG-nucleoporins: the flexible proteins that contribute to the nuclear pore’s selective barrier. I hope to understand how their spatial organization and motion relate to transport through individual pores.

My developing research interests bring together cell biology, structural imaging, and live-cell single-molecule measurements.

I’m a member of the Yang Lab, where research connects nucleocytoplasmic transport, single-molecule biophysics, and super-resolution microscopy. This is the setting for my doctoral training and developing research questions.
